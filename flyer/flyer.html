Building AI agents as digital colleagues for business productivity.

It does the work, not just the talking.

Most people have only ever used AI as a chat window that gives advice. Over two days you will set one up to actually open your files, build the report, and save it into your folder — finished work, not suggestions you still have to type up yourself.

What changes on Monday morning
01

The report that ate your day

The monthly pack, the management report, the board deck — drafted from your own figures in minutes, so your time goes on checking the numbers instead of keying them in.

02

No coding. No jargon.

If you can use email and Excel, you can do this. You give instructions in ordinary English, and every technical word is explained in plain language before it is used.

03

Real files, not text on a screen

Excel workbooks with working formulas. Word documents in your house style. Slide decks with speaker notes. Saved straight into your own folder, ready to send.

04

You are still the one who signs

It takes away the typing, not the judgement. You will know exactly what to check, and exactly what your AI colleague must never be trusted to decide.
