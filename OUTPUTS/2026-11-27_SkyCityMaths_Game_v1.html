    HOW TO PLAY

    • Fly: drag your finger or mouse anywhere on the screen, or use the arrow keys / WASD.
    • Shoot: your jet fires by itself. Just dodge the glowing bullets!
    • Power-ups: grab the glowing capsules. G Twin Guns, L Laser, M Missiles, S Shield. Capsules change letter, so grab the one you want.
    • Maths twist: every capsule asks a question. Right answer = that weapon goes UP 1 level. Wrong answer = you lose 1 level.
    • Mix weapons: guns, laser and missiles all fire together. Each goes up to Level 4.
    • Mega Bomb: tap BOMB or press Space to wipe out enemy bullets.
    • Free gifts: B extra bomb, ♥ extra heart.
    • Win: beat the boss at the end of all 5 stages. You have 3 hearts, so be careful!
